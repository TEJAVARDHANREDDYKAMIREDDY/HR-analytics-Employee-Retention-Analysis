# HR Analytics Employee Retention Analysis

## Overview

This project focuses on Employee Retention and Attrition Analysis using HR workforce data. The objective is to identify key factors influencing employee turnover and provide actionable insights to support workforce planning and retention strategies.

The solution combines SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau to perform data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and reporting.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition and retention trends.
- Evaluate workforce demographics and employment patterns.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition rates.
- Assess the impact of factors such as income, business travel, work-life balance, and distance from home.
- Create interactive dashboards for data-driven HR decision-making.

## Business Questions Answered

### Employee Overview
- What is the total employee count?
- What is the current active employee count?
- What is the overall attrition rate?

### Department Analysis
- Which departments have the highest attrition rate?
- Which departments have the longest average tenure?

### Demographic Analysis
- How does attrition vary across age groups?
- How does marital status impact attrition?
- How does attrition differ by job level?

### Workforce Behavior Analysis
- What is the relationship between business travel and attrition?
- How does distance from home influence employee turnover?
- How does work-life balance vary across job roles?

### Compensation Analysis
- How does monthly income affect employee retention?
- Which income groups experience the highest attrition?
